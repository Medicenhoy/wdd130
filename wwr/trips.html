Salmon River (Beginner)

Perfect for families and first-timers. Enjoy the scenic beauty of the Salmon River with gentle rapids and plenty of time for swimming and relaxation. Lunch is included on the riverbank.

Desolation Canyon (Intermediate)

A step up in excitement! Navigate through the rugged beauty of Desolation Canyon. This trip features Class II and III rapids, offering a mix of thrill and calm waters. Camping gear provided.

Grand Canyon (Advanced)

The ultimate whitewater experience. Challenge the legendary rapids of the Grand Canyon. This multi-day expedition requires physical stamina and a thirst for adrenaline. Experience of a lifetime.
